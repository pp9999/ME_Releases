# ⚡ AIO Empty Throne Room (AI SLOP STYLE)
> **Automated 10x Empowered Agility & Divination training with auto-switching & persistent tracking.**
### 🚀 What it does
* **🏃 Agility Mode (Auto-cycles)**:
  * Prioritizes the **10x Empowered cycle** with humanized dismount step-offs.
  * Auto-recharges Silverhawk Boots from inventory.
  * Stops cleanly at the 800k XP lifetime cap.
* **🔮 Divination Mode (Senntisten Crystals)**:
  * Transmutes raw crystals to the **10x Empowered color** & auto-deposits.
  * Mid-craft cancel & auto-recolor if the empowered crate shifts during crafting.
  * Handles multi-colored crystal inventories automatically.
* **📜 Clutter Free**: Automatically destroys Senntisten scrolls when found.
### 🎛️ Settings & GUI
* **Prioritize Empowered (10x)**: Always tracks and hops to glowing nodes.
* **Auto-stop at 800k Cap** *(Agility)*: Stops script when cap is reached.
* **Recharge Silverhawk Boots** *(Agility)*: Auto-refuels boots on low charges.
* **Min / Max Reaction (ms)**: Sliders for humanized reaction delays.
* **Controls**: `Start / Pause`, `Reset Session`, and `Switch Skill`.
### ✨ Highlights
* **🧠 Persistent JSON**: Auto-saves your 800k Agility progress & GUI settings across sessions/accounts in the script folder.
* **🔒 Streamer Safe**: Hides username from GUI so you never leak your name in screenshots.
* **📊 Live Dashboard**: Real-time XP/hr, runtime, level gains, and swaps counter.
### 📋 Setup & Requirements
1. **Reqs**: 65+ Agility / 65+ Divination.
2. Stand inside **The Empty Throne Room**.
3. Launch script ➜ Pick **Agility** or **Divination** ➜ Configure ➜ Hit **Start**!
